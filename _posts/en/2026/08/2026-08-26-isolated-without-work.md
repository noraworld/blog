I usually write a diary where I jot down daily events and thoughts, so I haven't had the opportunity to express my emotions through a blog before. My diary tends to be messy since it's not meant for others to see. So, I thought it might not be a bad idea to organize my thoughts better and share them with others to help release some unease, which is why I'm writing this now.

As I already shared in my previous blog, [Getting Fired from My Dream Job with a Complete Four-Day Work Week, Lasting Less Than a Month](https://note.com/noraworld/n/n354e153245aa), I landed what I thought was my dream job of working three days a week and planned to write on my free days. But it didn't even last a month before the contract ended, and since then, my days have been frustratingly stagnant.

Since May, I've been on the hunt for a new job but to no avail. It's become apparent that whether I work three days a week or more, if the company's environment doesn't suit me, my motivation dwindles. Having realized this, I have widened my search criteria beyond just working three days a week. Despite this, no offers have come through. I've been to about ten interviews, but I've been rejected by all.

The main reason, I think, is my recent employment history. It shows a pattern of short stints at various jobs, making me seem unreliable. After quitting a stable job and making a failed move to Australia with no plan, I returned to Japan and started freelancing. Still, I couldn't stick with one project very long, and when contracts ended, I drifted into periods of doing nothing. The current period of joblessness is one of those times.

Today is August 26, 2026. I've been searching for work since early May, nearly four months now, with no luck. With nearly no social connections, I've tried to stave off loneliness during this income-less time by losing my virginity at a brothel for the first time in my life, as written in [Losing My Virginity](https://note.com/noraworld/n/n6575a8ee534c). I bought a Nintendo Switch 2 a month after but seldom play it. I'm not sure what I want anymore. I should be actively looking for a job, but when things repeatedly don't work out, it's easy to lose hope and motivation. Even if I find a job, I worry that I won't be able to maintain that motivation. I am questioning if forcing myself to keep working is worth living for.

Despite being unemployed, I also waste money, and while I receive demands for tax and insurance payments, I can't afford them. If I pay them all now, my living expenses will run out quickly as I don't know when I'll work again. It's putting me in a precarious situation. This pattern happened last year and the year before. I find a job, then quickly quit, struggle financially, and repeat the cycle.

I vowed last year to escape this unstable lifestyle and work seriously, but here I am again. It's hard for people to change so easily, I suppose.

Since graduating college, I've worked as a web engineer. However, with AI coding now dominating, the ability to write code alone isn't enough to get hired anymore. Insufficient work history is also a reason, but being replaced by AI in coding jobs makes the situation ironic. I thought tech jobs were safe from automation for longer, but this might actually be one of the first to go. Engineers who can leverage AI are fine, but with my periods of unemployment and lag in technical skills and AI usage, I feel it might be impossible to continue as an engineer.

Still, having worked as an engineer my whole career except for part-time jobs during school, I can't think of other work I could do. I need to find a company to attach myself to before I completely run out of money. Freelancing is unstable, so returning to a permanent position might be better for avoiding frequent job changes. Recently, I've started looking at full-time positions again in parallel with freelance gigs.

Freelancing was my only focus for the past few years, partly out of habit and because contract jobs usually require fewer interviews. However, even after nearly four months of searching, I have no luck, leading me to consider full-time jobs despite knowing it will be tougher. I'm proceeding with both options, planning to accept whichever opportunity arises first, but I wonder if finding a job or exhausting my savings will happen first.

An agent advised me to update my resume, and logically, I know I should spend time improving my resume and applying for jobs. But with a defeatist mindset, I find it hard to get myself to act.

There's no real conclusion to this article, no goals or hope for improvement. I'm living on out of fear of dying, feeling guilty about being a burden. I regret past neglect and not visiting my late grandmother more often. With my difficult relationship with my parents, I never went back for the funeral. Even though they financially supported me during school, and my grandmother expressed a wish to meet me, I didn't return home or attend her funeral later. These regrets and guilt feel overwhelming now.

With my parents, I once thought my attempts to mend things were sincere, but maybe I was just stubborn, thinking they were at fault. Reflecting now, I wonder if reconciliation might have been possible, and that uncertainty weighs on me. If that estrangement also cut me off from my grandmother, I feel lost in remorse and guilt.

Writing this blog was supposed to help me sort through and express recent frustrations, but that didn't happen. Instead, I've become even more uncertain about what I want to say or achieve. Nothing seems important anymore.

Speaking of social connections, I recently lost touch even with a friend I occasionally met. Although I sent a birthday message via LINE, the response was cold. My friend isn't a typical worker but lives off app ad revenue, requiring less active work. Meanwhile, I can't afford long periods without an income, so I must work as an employee (including freelance).

This drift in our life circumstances might be driving us apart, with my friend communicating less. Without a job, no family contact, and losing my last social bond, I'm feeling isolated. When it gets to this point, I wonder if it's the end for me.
